DMG install window

Today the DMG ships electron-builder's default grey background with a dashed arrow, and no window size, so Finder opens it huge. Each option is a 660x400 background image plus fixed icon positions. Finder draws the icons and their labels on top of the art. Labels are drawn black: your screenshot shows Finder in dark mode still drawing black labels on the light default background.

O1 dark, the icon's gradient

Strongest identity, the window looks like the app. Black labels on near-black, so this only works if Finder flips label colour on dark art. Needs a real build to confirm.

O2 light, flat white

Matches the light theme and the no-shadow rule. Labels always readable. Least identity.

O3 split, dark band + light floor

Brand lives in the band, icons and labels sit on #f4f6f8. Identity of O1, readability of O2. Recommended.
